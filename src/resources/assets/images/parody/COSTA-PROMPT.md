# 코스타 델 솔 배경

- 생성 도구: 내장 `image_gen` (스타일 변환, 불투명 배경).
- 입력 1: 사용자가 첨부한 Costa del Sol 스크린샷. 지형·건축 참고.
- 입력 2: 기존 타타루 배구 경기 화면. 도트 밀도와 그래픽 스타일 참고.
- 생성 원본: 프로젝트의 `art/source/costa-del-sol-pixel.png`.
- 실제 게임 에셋: `src/resources/assets/images/parody/costa-del-sol-native.png`. 렌더러가 변환한 432×248 픽셀·32색 결과를 PNG로 저장해 로딩 용량을 줄였습니다.
- 게임 적용: `parody.js`에서 432×248 픽셀, 32색 팔레트, 최근접 보간으로 변환. 원작 코트·네트 위의 풍경만 교체합니다. 원작 구름 네 개를 하늘 위쪽에 작게 배치하며 파도와 경기 요소는 기존 렌더러를 사용합니다.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Use case: style-transfer. Asset: an actual background bitmap for the existing 1997 Windows Pikachu Volleyball fan game, replacing ONLY the scenery above the unchanged volleyball court. Input 1 is the user's Costa del Sol (Final Fantasy XIV) screenshot, a SUBJECT AND ARCHITECTURE reference. Input 2 is the CURRENT GAME SCREEN, a strict PIXEL DENSITY / SIMPLE RETRO STYLE reference only; DO NOT copy its characters, ball, scores, net, UI, letters, court, or busy rainbow noise. Create only a clean wide landscape background, 1728x992 output representing a native 432x248 pixel image enlarged 4 times with nearest-neighbor hard square pixels. It must look like a simple hand-drawn 1990s 16-color/32-color Windows game backdrop, NOT a modern highly detailed illustration, not a photo converted with a pixel filter. Very limited flat colors, thick stepped silhouettes, sparse ordered dithering in shadows only, simple readable shapes, no antialiasing, no blur, no painterly shading, no smooth gradients or glow. Recognizable Costa del Sol details: turquoise lagoon and blue sea, pale cream sand, ochre-brown elevated wooden boardwalks on stilts, palm trees, signature narrow tall white sail-like tent canopies with thatched bases, rocky green-topped coastal cliffs on the left, and ONE small cyan faceted aetheryte crystal in a dark gold ring on the left third. Main white tent resort tower on the RIGHT edge, smaller canopies/decks along the bottom right. Composition is a side-view distant backdrop for a volleyball game: upper HALF is mostly plain solid medium-blue sky, with NO CLOUDS (the game will draw animated clouds); ocean horizon at about 62% height. Keep the central corridor and the top corners visually calm so the moving ball and red scoreboard remain readable. Architecture clustered in the lower third and far right; tallest right-hand tent tip may rise to 42% height. The left cliff reaches no higher than 60% height. Aetheryte is a small simple background landmark around x=28%, y=75%, NOT a giant central object. Foreground bottom 8% is a quiet horizontal pale sandy strip ending flat along the entire bottom edge, ready to meet the original court. Keep almost all details below 60% height. Flat 2D game scenery, sunny and cheerful, modest muted background colors so the black-outlined yellow Yan characters will stand out. No people, animals, characters, ball, volleyball equipment, scoreboard, title, logo, UI, text, watermark, borders, frames, or transparent margins. Fully opaque rectangular background.
